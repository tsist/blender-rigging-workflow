# Validation and quality status

This release publishes existing self-authored code and portable packaging. Detailed rigging/deformation quality has not been accepted. Historical project checks are evidence for their own inputs, not public cross-character guarantees.

Prior development includes real control/bone responses, constrained bone edits, local weight edits, expression previews, copy-save/reopen/recovery and selected hair-bone physics tests. Some component UI operators were exercised in background Blender; that is distinct from a complete visible workflow. Candidate elbow/hand improvements with insufficient visible benefit were not adopted.

Proportion-control linkage has a finite working study, while its mixed UI trial was cancelled before a final conclusion. Garment import/preparation produced candidates, but fitting remained unaccepted; the final surface-capture trial failed and produced no saved candidate. These studies are included as developer source, not polished released features.

For actual rig inspection use a few large-angle cyclic movements that expose defects: splits, bent elbows, squats and appropriate hand poses. Observe intermediate frames and the intended moving region. Face tests must be chosen for the specific character. Hair physics remains in scope and must be observed for the actual rig and collision inputs.

Do not turn historical precision, test counts or performance numbers into a universal acceptance checklist. Preserve the source and non-target data, verify meaningful save/reopen/recovery behavior, and select checks for the actual change. Functional response and detailed quality are separate outcomes.

Release-specific checks and their observed results are recorded in `PUBLICATION.json` after execution. Registration/import or archive checks do not certify skinning quality, third-party capability integration, GUI usability, physics quality or other platforms.
